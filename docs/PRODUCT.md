# Product contract — abbreviated

**Sira Ba? — Picture-an. Presyuhan. Paayos.** Filipino neighborhoods' affordable small repairs through photo-first requests, multiple itemized worker quotes, category-verified workers, explicit customer approvals, evidence and support for incomplete/poor work. Allowed V1 categories: minor plumbing, ordinary clogs and low-risk fixtures. Don't promise instant matching, a guaranteed fix, insurance, hazardous category coverage or lowest price.

First public pilot (after compliance and QA) has **zero Sira Ba? platform/booking fees**. Customers pay workers offline for agreed repairs. Proposed *later* customer-paid booking charge is 5% of approved LABOR (not materials/travel), and requires separate legal/payment/operational authorization. Worker-credit precedent is superseded. See immutable Master Blueprint v1.0 for complete authority.
